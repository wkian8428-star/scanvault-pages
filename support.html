Quick Start

  • 📷 Scan a document: Tap the + button, position your document, and tap the capture button
  • ✂️ Crop & adjust: Drag the corner handles to fine-tune the document edges
  • 🔍 OCR text: After scanning, tap "Text" to extract text from your document
  • 📄 Export PDF: Tap Export and choose PDF format — no watermarks
  • 📁 Organize: Use folders to organize your scanned documents

Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is my data private?

Yes. 100% of your data is stored locally on your device. We have no servers, no cloud sync, and no data collection.

💳 How do I unlock all features?

ScanVault is a one-time purchase. Tap "Unlock" in the app and complete your purchase. No subscription, ever.

📱 Does it work offline?

Yes! All features work completely offline. No internet connection required.

🔄 How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchase. If you previously purchased, your unlock will be restored.

📄 Why are my scanned documents blurry?

Make sure the camera lens is clean and hold your device steady. Use the flash button in low-light conditions.

🌐 Does it support multiple languages?

Yes! OCR supports 70+ languages including English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, and more.

📱 Which devices are supported?

ScanVault supports all iPhones and iPads running iOS 16 or later.

🔍 OCR doesn't recognize my text

Make sure the text is clear and well-lit. You can also select recognition languages in Settings → OCR Languages.

Troubleshooting

Camera not working: Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy → Camera → enable ScanVault

Purchase not recognized: Make sure you're using the same Apple ID you used to purchase

App crashes: Try restarting your device. Make sure you're running iOS 16 or later

PDF export fails: Ensure you have enough storage space on your device
