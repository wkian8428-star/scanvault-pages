🔒 Privacy Policy

ScanVault — Your documents stay on your device.

English 中文

1. Information We Collect

ScanVault does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

We do not use any analytics SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party services that collect user data.

The only data stored by ScanVault is stored locally on your device:

  • Scanned documents & images — stored in the app's private directory
  • OCR text — processed locally using Apple's on-device Vision framework
  • App settings — stored locally via UserDefaults
  • Purchase receipts — verified locally via StoreKit (no server contact)

2. How We Use Information

All document processing happens 100% on your device. Scanned images and extracted text never leave your iPhone or iPad. We have no access to your documents, photos, or personal information.

OCR uses Apple's on-device Vision framework — no cloud service is involved.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. ScanVault contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or third-party data processors. Your scanned documents remain completely private.

4. Third-Party Services

ScanVault does not integrate any third-party services that collect data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No cloud storage services
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services

5. Apple's Services

ScanVault uses the following Apple frameworks for on-device processing:

  • AVFoundation — Camera access for document scanning
  • Vision — On-device rectangle detection and OCR text recognition
  • PDFKit — Local PDF generation
  • StoreKit — Local purchase verification

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. To delete your data:

  • Simply delete the ScanVault app — all local data is removed with it
  • To delete individual documents: open the app → select documents → delete

We have no servers, so we have no data to delete on our end.

7. Children's Privacy

ScanVault does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our app is a document scanning tool with no content directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
